# Privacy Policy — Shooting Tracker

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026

Shooting Tracker ("the App") is developed and maintained by **AZ-Labs** ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Information You Provide

Shooting Tracker allows you to record and manage information related to your shooting activities, including:

- Training sessions and shooting results
- Performance statistics and progress
- Personal training goals
- Notes and other information you choose to enter

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the App's functionality.

We do not automatically transmit your training records to our servers.

## 2. Data Storage and Processing

The App is designed to operate primarily on your device.

Training records and related information are stored and processed locally. We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

If data export or sharing features are available, you control whether to export or share your information and which destination to use.

Device operating systems and backup services may independently manage copies of locally stored data according to your device settings and the applicable service provider's policies.

## 3. Analytics and Diagnostic Information

We may introduce optional analytics or diagnostic technologies in future versions of the App to understand usage patterns, identify technical issues, and improve reliability.

Any such technologies that collect information from your device will be disclosed in this Privacy Policy when implemented, including the relevant data categories, purposes, and third-party service providers.

## 4. Purchases and Premium Features

The App may offer optional paid features in future versions.

If purchases are made available, payment transactions may be processed through the relevant app store or payment provider. We will not directly request or store your complete payment card details.

Information required to verify purchases or manage access to paid features will be handled as described in the updated version of this Privacy Policy, where applicable.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on platform services provided by Android, Google Play, or other service providers.

These services may operate under their own privacy policies.

If we integrate third-party services that collect or process information on our behalf, we will identify the relevant providers and describe their data practices in this Privacy Policy as required.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Training records stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App's local data.

You can remove locally stored information using the available App functionality or your device's application storage controls.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its local application data. However, previously exported files or copies retained by device backup services may remain subject to your device settings and the policies of those services.

We do not maintain server-side copies of your training records under the App's current local-storage functionality.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Locally stored data is subject to the security features of your device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general sports training recordkeeping and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires parental consent or other legal safeguards, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

## 9. Contacting Us

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, and address technical issues.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for these purposes or to satisfy applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to applicable requirements.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, its functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements.
